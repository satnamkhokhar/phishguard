THE BASICS

Three things to watch for

Look closely at the URL

Fake links can use familiar brand names. Always check the actual domain.

EXAMPLEpaypal-login.example.comThe domain is example.com, not PayPal.

Quick tip: Visit official websites directly.

Don't fall for urgency

Urgent messages about locked accounts or passwords are common phishing tricks.

EXAMPLE“Your account expires in 10 minutes. Verify NOW!”Urgency is a warning sign.

Quick tip: Verify requests independently.

Build safer habits

Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication (MFA).

REMEMBERHTTPS encrypts traffic but does not prove a website is trustworthy.

Quick tip: Avoid unexpected attachments and login links.
