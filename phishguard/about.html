About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is a web-based application designed to help users identify potentially suspicious or malicious website links. The goal of the application is to make it easier for everyday internet users to recognize common warning signs associated with phishing websites and understand why a link may be considered suspicious.

Why I Created PhishGuard

I decided to create PhishGuard because phishing is one of the most common types of online scams. Suspicious links can appear through emails, text messages, social media, and other websites, and they are not always easy to recognize. I wanted to create an application that could eventually give users a simple way to enter a URL and receive information about possible security risks.

My Motivation

As a computer science student, I was also motivated to create a project that connects web development with cybersecurity. Throughout the semester, I plan to expand PhishGuard by adding features such as URL analysis, risk scores, scan history, and database storage. In the future, I would also like to explore machine learning and browser-based phishing detection.
